In August I was made redundant. My time is my own now and I
want to improve my world and the world of the people around me.
Here are my main tracks for the rest of this year:

## music

I play piano and bass every day. I will practise these and aim at improving
my playing not just standing still. I haven't been playing guitar every day but I will
begin to do so, and use that as my step into making music for people to see,
by playing at open mic nights. I will write some songs.

## fixing and decorating my house

My house is a mix of elements that I've put work into so they're how I like
them and elements that have been waiting for me to do that, some for over 20
years now. There are still too few of the former and too many of the latter.
I will work towards making the whole place better, more to my own
actual liking and more like something I would hope people drop round to
rather than something I fear the shame of people seeing.

## making clothes

I've made clothes and costumes before, for myself and others, but never
taken it seriously enough to be good at it. Now I will take it seriously.
In the last few weeks, I've tried to find a pattern drafting course to take;
two courses were cancelled between my applying for them and the course
starting. I will learn the skills I'm missing myself, starting with books
and the Internet.

Designing and making clothes for a specific human being combines
the technical, the aesthetic, and the personal. Being able to start with a
conversation and a tape measure and finish with someone walking away thinking
"I feel great in this" is a microcosm of the way I want to interact with the
world. I will find people to make things for. By the end of the year I want
to have made things for 10 different people. I'm actively looking for
collaborators: if you're reading this and you know me or live near me, I'd
like to make you something.
